About the Film

“Success has never been a single path.”

Samagra follows the journey of two children who grow up under completely different expectations, yet are pushed toward the same destination — success.

Lok Pradeep is raised to believe that academic excellence is the only key to a successful future, while Raghu Nandan discovers his purpose through technology, coding, hackathons, creativity, and real-world experiences. As they step into a highly competitive world, their contrasting paths begin to reveal unexpected truths about learning, growth, and opportunity.

As their lives unfold, Samagra asks a powerful question: What truly matters in today's world?

The film explores the gap between generational expectations and the rapidly changing demands of modern society. It reminds parents that marks alone should never define a child's potential, and encourages students to discover their passion, build practical skills, and grow beyond the classroom.

More than a film about education, Samagra is a story about dreams, family, passion, and the evolving meaning of success. Because the world is changing, and the way we prepare the next generation must change with it.
